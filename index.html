Institutional Overview

The Global Synergetic Foundation (GSFN) — also known as GSFN, GSF Network and GSFN Bharat — is an independent interdisciplinary research and publishing initiative established in 1992 and restructured in 2007, 2014 and 2024 in New Delhi, India.

The foundation provides an intellectual platform supporting long-term scholarship that examines structural relationships between classical knowledge traditions and contemporary scientific inquiry. It encourages research integrating philosophical analysis, textual interpretation and formal reasoning to address foundational questions concerning knowledge, cognition, cosmology and the conceptual structure of scientific thought.

Institutional Purpose

GSFN supports independent scholarship that bridges classical intellectual traditions and modern theoretical sciences through interdisciplinary research combining philosophy, textual studies, mathematics and cognitive inquiry.

Publishing & Scholarly Communication

Research is disseminated through the Astitva publication series, an interdisciplinary programme supporting monographs, micro-monographs and scholarly research communications.
